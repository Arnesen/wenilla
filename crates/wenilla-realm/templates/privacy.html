Privacy

This realm stores as little about you as it can:

  • Your login: the username and display name the admin gave you, a salted hash of your password, and your sign-in sessions.
  • Your web address: the IP you signed in from, kept for 90 days for abuse handling, then removed.
  • Your characters: everything the game itself keeps (characters, items, chat is not logged). The game server never sees your IP — it only sees this website.
  • The audit log: what admins did (bans, resets) and when you signed in.

Nothing is shared with anyone else, and nothing is sold. To have your account and characters deleted, ask the admin — deletion is immediate and complete.
